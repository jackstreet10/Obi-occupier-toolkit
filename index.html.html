01 / Discover

Booking.com appointed OBI to define the full occupational requirement for its new Manchester hub, then secure the right building. Our targeted market search identified Manchester Goods Yard for its scale, sustainability credentials and ability to support a 175,000 sq ft consolidation. OBI negotiated Heads of Terms (HoTs) and managed the full legal process, including Licence to Alter (LTA), Schedule of Condition (SoC) and connectivity due diligence, through to completion.

02 / Dream

We developed a workplace strategy aligned to Booking.com's global culture and collaboration-led model. The interior design narrative drew inspiration from the seven continents, with each floor themed around a different region. Highlights included a 250-person auditorium, fully connected meeting suites, zen rooms, breakout zones, games rooms and external winter gardens on every floor, all designed to foster creativity, connection and wellbeing.

03 / Execute

OBI delivered Manchester's largest Category B (CAT B) fit-out in eight years, an £18.5m project delivered end-to-end through our integrated Building Consultancy team. From project management and contract administration to surveys, due diligence and dilapidations strategy, every element was managed under one roof.

A future-proof platform for Booking.com's continued growth in Manchester, delivered through OBI's integrated Discover → Dream → Execute model.

The Beauty Tech Group
16,600 sq ft Alderley Park expansion. Design, delivery & advisory
View details →
+
1 / 3
01 / Discover

Following earlier retail and workspace collaborations in Cheshire, OBI was instructed to support The Beauty Tech Group with the expansion of their existing workspace within Alderley Park as the business scaled toward its £300m+ Initial Public Offering (IPO).

The brief covered team growth, hybrid patterns, Research and Development (R&D), content creation and brand expression. Alderley Park's Glasshouse was identified for its scalability and innovation ecosystem; OBI negotiated favourable Heads of Terms (HoTs) and managed the full transaction through to completion.

02 / Dream

A wellbeing‑led design language shaped directly by The Beauty Tech Group's products: curved forms, reflective finishes and a warm, feminine aesthetic.

Our scope spanned concept design through to Fixtures, Furniture and Equipment (FF&E) specification, dressing and planting, creating a space that balanced brand expression, wellbeing and operational efficiency.

03 / Execute

OBI delivered the full CAT B fit‑out, integrating Forest Stewardship Council (FSC)‑certified, low‑Volatile Organic Compound (VOC) and recycled finishes, Light Emitting Diode (LED) lighting (~40% energy reduction), biophilic planting, and a dedicated wellbeing room with a Pulsed Electromagnetic Field (PEMF) mat.

A future‑ready, human‑centric workspace supporting a £300m+ business and a predominantly female workforce, recognised with the 2025 Northern Design Award.
